US 2040: Strategic Futures

This interactive matrix supports an ongoing research project. It was originally designed without any particular country in mind. I'm now exploring how its two dimensions might help us think about the United States in 2040.

Translucence: How deeply civilian and military institutions, identities, responsibilities and social relationships overlap.

Strategic Tenacity: How effectively a society sustains and adapts collective effort in pursuit of meaningful strategic objectives.

This is an early, exploratory exercise.
